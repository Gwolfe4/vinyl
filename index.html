Connect Spotify

This takes about five minutes, once. You only need your normal Spotify login.

  1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with Spotify. Accept the terms if asked.
  2. Tap Create app. Name it Vinyl, and type anything for the description.
  3. In Redirect URIs, paste this address and tap Add:
  4. Under “Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?”, check Web API. Agree to the terms and tap Save.
